Who is EduOp CRM built for?+

It is designed for coaching centres, training institutes and education teams managing enquiries, admissions, students, fees and follow-ups.

Can multiple team members use it?+

Yes. Different roles can work in the same system with access based on their responsibilities.

Can we track pending fees and instalments?+

Yes. The CRM keeps collection history, pending amounts and student-level fee details organised.

Will it work on mobile devices?+

Yes. The interface is designed to remain usable across desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
